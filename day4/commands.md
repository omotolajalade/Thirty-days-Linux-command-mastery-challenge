1. find -name
Searches for a file or directory by its name.
2. find -type
Searches for something based on its type, such as a file or directory.
3. find -size
Searches for files based on their size, such as files larger or smaller than a certain size.
4. find -mtime
Searches for files based on when they were last modified.
5. find -perm
Searches for files based on their permissions, such as 644 or 777.
6. locate
Quickly searches for files and directories using a database of their locations.
7. updatedb
Updates the database that the locate command uses to find files.
8. du
Shows how much disk space files and directories are using.
9. du -sh
Shows the total disk space used by a directory in an easy-to-read format.
10. df -h
Shows the total, used, and available disk space on your filesystem in an easy-to-read format.
