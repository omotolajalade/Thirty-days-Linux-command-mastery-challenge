 Day 24 — Deeper Service Management & Logs

Today I learned how to manage Linux services more deeply and investigate system logs. I practised finding failed services, reloading systemd configuration, and using journalctl to view and filter service logs.

I also learned how to view logs from a specific service, filter for errors, view logs from a particular time, and continuously monitor new log entries using journalctl -f and tail -f.

what surprised me was Logs are essential for troubleshooting because they help me understand what happened on a Linux system and identify errors or service problems.
